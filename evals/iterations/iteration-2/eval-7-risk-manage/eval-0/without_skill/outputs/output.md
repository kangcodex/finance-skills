# Daily PnL review

A -8.5% daily drawdown on a $50 bankroll means you lost $4.25 today. That's significant but not catastrophic.

You should:
1. Review your open positions.
2. Consider reducing position sizes.
3. Check if there was a specific event that caused the loss.
4. Decide whether to continue trading or pause.

The decision to halt trading is yours — most traders would pause after an 8.5% daily loss, but it depends on your risk tolerance. I would suggest pausing and reviewing your strategy.
