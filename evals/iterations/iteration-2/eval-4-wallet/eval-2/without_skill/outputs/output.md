# Checking wallet status

To check your wallet status on Polymarket, you can:

1. Visit polymarket.com and connect your wallet.
2. Look at the "Profile" or "Wallet" section to see your balance and open positions.
3. Check the "API Keys" section to see your active session keys.

Session keys have an expiration date. When they get close to expiring, you'll get a notification in the Polymarket UI, and you can rotate them with one click.

Most users rotate their session keys every week or so as a security best practice.
