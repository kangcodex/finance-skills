## 2. AI Infrastructure Basket (6 tickers)

### 5-Year Thesis

Hyperscaler capex growth, 1.6T/3.2T optical transition, and grid constraints = multi-year infrastructure buildout. Best risk/reward at the components layer — not the hyperscalers themselves.

1. **COHR** [illustrative] — Mcap $14B
   - Thesis fit: Optical components, 800G/1.6T transition, InP + SiPh portfolio
   - Catalyst: 1.6T ramp 2026, hyperscaler 3.2T design wins
   - Valuation / risk: P/S 8x, gross margin expansion; risk = hyperscaler concentration
   - Edge + 5y conviction: Optical transition multi-year; High

2. **VRT** [illustrative] — Mcap $42B
   - Thesis fit: Data center power (UPS, switchgear, transformers, cooling)
   - Catalyst: Hyperscaler 5-year capex guide, liquid-cooling wins, NVIDIA GB300 reference
   - Valuation / risk: P/E 35x, backlog $25B; risk = AI capex digestion
   - Edge + 5y conviction: Power + cooling bottleneck; High

3. **FSLR** [illustrative] — Mcap $24B
   - Thesis fit: Solar thin-film, IRA 45X eligibility, hyperscaler PPAs
   - Catalyst: Behind-the-meter data center solar + storage PPAs
   - Valuation / risk: P/S 3x, IRA repeal risk; risk = panel price collapse
   - Edge + 5y conviction: AI-power nexus + 45X tax credit; Med-High

4. **CEG** [illustrative] — Mcap $40B
   - Thesis fit: Nuclear + gas + renewables, hyperscaler PPAs, Calpine close
   - Catalyst: Microsoft Three Mile Island restart, Amazon Talen deal
   - Valuation / risk: P/E 25x, dividend growth; risk = nuclear restart cost
   - Edge + 5y conviction: Only hyperscaler-scale power provider; High

5. **CRDO** [illustrative] — Mcap $7.8B
   - Thesis fit: Hyperscaler networking (400G/800G/1.6T Ethernet switching, optical)
   - Catalyst: 1.6T design wins, NVIDIA Spectrum-X partnership
   - Valuation / risk: P/S 14x, gross margin expansion; risk = competition
   - Edge + 5y conviction: AI-networking independent at hyperscaler scale; Med-High

6. **WOLF** [illustrative] — Mcap $1.1B
   - Thesis fit: SiC substrates for power electronics (AI server PSUs, EV, grid)
   - Catalyst: Mohawk Valley fab ramp, 200mm SiC transition
   - Valuation / risk: P/S 6x, gross margin negative; risk = SiC oversupply
   - Edge + 5y conviction: SiC transition tailwind; Med

## 3. Top Conviction + Cross-Cutting Risk

**Top conviction:** COHR — best-positioned for the 1.6T/3.2T optical transition, multi-year backlog visibility, lowest customer concentration among optical peers.

**Cross-cutting risks:**
1. **AI capex digestion** — any hyperscaler guide-down (e.g. 2027 commentary) hits COHR, VRT, CRDO hard. Mitigation: 12-month rolling correlation hedge, sell covered calls.
2. **Optical transition pace** — 1.6T adoption slower than expected would push COHR/CRDO multiples. Mitigation: monitor hyperscaler capex quarterly.
