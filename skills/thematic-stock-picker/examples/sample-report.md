## 3. Top 2–3 Convictions + Cross-Cutting Risks

**Top convictions (across all themes):**
1. **ASM** (Ga/Ge) — Only US producer of gallium and germanium, both export-controlled by China. DPA funding closes the capex gap. 5x+ from current if it executes — true 0-or-5-bagger.
2. **CDMO** (peptide CDMO) — Picks-and-shovels on the GLP-1 supercycle. LLY + NVO capacity constraint means $5B+ revenue at the platform layer by 2030.
3. **LHX** (defense missiles/EW) — Javelin + Stinger + Aerojet synergies + NATO munitions build. Backlog growth at 8-12% per year with operating leverage.

**Cross-cutting risks:**
1. **Sticky core services inflation** — if core PCE re-accelerates above 3.0%, the Fed delays cuts → duration trades unwind, growth multiples compress, AI leadership extends but at lower index level. Mitigation: keep 8-10% cash buffer, hedge SPY/SPX with 5% OTM puts.
2. **AI capex digestion** — any hyperscaler guide-down on AI capex (e.g. 2027 commentary) would hit SOXX, NVDA, AVGO, AMD, CRDO, COHR. Mitigation: 12-month rolling correlation hedge, sell covered calls on momentum names.
3. **China retaliation** — tariff escalation, export control expansion on critical minerals could benefit US producers (LTHM, MP, ASM) but hurt US companies with China revenue (CRDO, NVDA, AMD). Mitigation: stay US/EU/CA/AU/KR/JP domicile pure-plays, avoid any name with >15% China revenue.
4. **IRA / CHIPS repeal risk** — political shift could roll back the 45X/45Y/48E tax credits + CHIPS funding. Mitigation: diversify across industrial-policy-agnostic themes (defense, GLP-1) and rate-of-return-durable picks.
