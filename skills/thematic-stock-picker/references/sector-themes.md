# Sector Themes — 2026–2031 Policy-Anchored Catalog

A working catalog of high-conviction, policy-anchored 5-year themes the picker can draw from. Each theme names the **policy driver**, the **sectoral beneficiary archetype**, the **metric the screen should track**, and the **key risks**. Themes are organized by driver type: industrial policy, defense, energy, healthcare, AI, and macro/geopolitical.

For each theme, the picker should produce 5–8 surviving tickers. The catalog is **suggestive, not exhaustive** — the picker can also generate fresh themes that intersect current policy with a clear data trail.

## Industrial Policy / Reshoring

### Critical Minerals & Strategic Materials

- **Policy drivers:** US IRA (45X, 45Y, 48C, 48E), Defense Production Act for critical minerals (2022 invocation), EU Critical Raw Materials Act (2024), CHIPS precursor minerals, Japan's Economic Security Promotion Act.
- **Beneficiary archetype:** US/EU/CA/AU/JP-listed miners, refiners, recyclers of Li, Ni, Co, REE (Nd/Pr/Dy/Tb), Ga, Ge, Mn, W, graphite, lithium, copper.
- **Screen metrics:** jurisdiction mix, producing vs exploring, AISC, NPV, IRA 45X eligibility, capex funded.
- **Key risks:** commodity price beta, China retaliation on export controls, capex overruns, single-asset failure.
- **Agencies / sources:** USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries, DoE Loan Programs Office, EU CRM Act scoreboard.

### Semiconductors (advanced + mature + packaging)

- **Policy drivers:** CHIPS Act ($52.7B + 25% investment tax credit), CHIPS for America program office, EU Chips Act (€43B), Japan Rapidus + TSMC Kumamoto, Korea K-Chips Act, China Big Fund III.
- **Beneficiary archetype:** equipment (deposition, etch, lithography adjacents), EDA, IP, advanced packaging, HBM, SiC power, GaN, mature-node foundry.
- **Screen metrics:** CHIPS funding status, customer concentration, capex visibility, export-control exposure, China revenue.
- **Key risks:** export-control escalation, hyperscaler capex digestion, mature-node overcapacity, customer concentration (e.g. Apple).
- **Agencies / sources:** CHIPS.gov (NIST), SIA, SEMI, BIS export control list.

### Power Grid Capex (T&D, transformers, switchgear, BESS)

- **Policy drivers:** IRA 45X/48E, IIJA grid resilience funding ($73B), FERC Order 2023 (interconnection), state Integrated Resource Plans (IRPs), EU Net-Zero Industry Act, BESS mandates.
- **Beneficiary archetype:** transformer manufacturers, switchgear, HVDC vendors, BESS integrators, smart meter companies, grid software.
- **Screen metrics:** backlog, lead times, utility customer concentration, EEI capex plans.
- **Key risks:** transformer lead times (currently 18-24mo), permitting, copper/aluminum costs, utility ROE compression, hyperscaler behind-the-meter.
- **Agencies / sources:** EEI, FERC, DoE, Wood Mackenzie, S&P Global Platts.

### Industrials / Reshoring (machinery, automation, factories)

- **Policy drivers:** IRA build-out demand, CHICS-related capex, IIJA "Buy American", Defense Production Act, EU Made in Europe, India PLI scheme, Mexico nearshoring.
- **Beneficiary archetype:** US-domiciled machinery, automation & robotics, factory construction (EPCs), specialty industrial gases, machine tools.
- **Screen metrics:** announced vs commissioned capex, customer mix (industrial / defense / semis), pricing power, backlog.
- **Key risks:** labor cost, automation capex, cycle normalization, M&A premium baked in.
- **Agencies / sources:** NAM, Reshoring Initiative, FRED industrial production.

## How to use this catalog

When generating the 5 themes for a report:

1. **Check the macro/policy backdrop** in the prompt — what laws, regulations, geopolitical events are driving 2026–2031?
2. **Pick themes that intersect distinct policy drivers** — don't produce 5 themes all anchored to CHIPS/AI. Diversify across industrial policy, defense, energy, healthcare, AI, macro.
3. **Verify each theme has a screenable universe** — at least 10-20 public companies that pass the master screen.
4. **Verify each theme has a quantifiable catalyst** — a 12-24 month event that re-rates the basket.
5. **Aim for sector diversity** — 5 themes from 5 different sectors is the target. If you must overlap, do it deliberately.
6. **Cite the policy driver** in the thesis, with a URL in the Sources block.

The catalog is updated as the policy cycle evolves. Treat it as a living document, not a fixed list.
